About us

Leafwork started as a simple idea: students want to help, but the information is scattered across society posters, WhatsApp groups and word of mouth. We bring those listings into one place.

We work to get more students involved in social work, support the nonprofits that run these drives, spread environmental awareness, back education initiatives and make volunteering easier to reach.

What we focus on

  • Student participation in social work
  • Support for nonprofit organizations
  • Environmental awareness
  • Educational initiatives
  • Accessible volunteering
